# Questions to Answer Before Starting the Job Search

Answer these once, then reuse the answers to fill in Prompt 1 / Prompt 2 from
`CLAUDE.md` every time you run the workflow.

## Role & scope
1. What role/title are you targeting? (e.g. "Marketing Manager", "Backend Developer")
2. Any adjacent/alternate titles to also search under? (companies word roles differently)
3. What industry or type of company, if any? (startup, MNC, agency, product vs. service, specific sector)
4. What level are you targeting? (individual contributor, team lead, manager)
4a. Are you targeting multiple roles at once? If so, list them — and decide whether they're
    similar enough to combine into one search table, or different enough to run separately
    (see "Multiple roles" in `CLAUDE.md`).

## Experience & eligibility
5. How many years of experience do you have?
6. What experience range should the search use? (e.g. "3 to 5 years")
7. Any certifications, degrees, or visa/work-authorization constraints that affect eligibility?

## Location & work mode
8. Which city/cities (or "remote") are you searching in?
9. Are you open to relocation? If so, where?
10. Remote, hybrid, or onsite — or any of the three?

## Compensation & constraints
11. What's your minimum acceptable salary / expected salary range?
12. Any notice period or availability start date to mention/filter by?
13. Any companies or industries you want to explicitly exclude (past employer, competitors, ethical concerns)?

## Search freshness & sources
14. How far back should listings go? (e.g. "posted this week")
15. Any specific companies whose career pages you already want checked?
16. Any job boards/sources to include or exclude beyond the default (skip LinkedIn/Indeed, prefer company career pages)?

## Resume & materials
17. Is your resume ready as a PDF to attach for Prompt 2 (skill-gap matching)?
18. Is the resume tailored to the target role, or generic?
19. Do you have a portfolio, LinkedIn profile, or GitHub to reference in outreach/applications?

## Cadence & output
20. How often do you want this search re-run? (e.g. daily, every morning)
21. Do you want results as a table in-chat, or exported (Excel/CSV)?
22. Do you want a running log of applied-to jobs to avoid duplicates over time?
